Frog Run – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Frog Run is designed as a simple game and does not collect, store, or share personal information from players.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Frog Run ("the Game") handles information. We respect your privacy and have designed the Game so that it does not require you to provide personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Frog Run does not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Frog Run saves your game progress locally on your own device, such as your coins, unlocked skins, missions progress, high score, and settings (sound, haptic feedback, and difficulty). This information stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any third party, and does not identify you. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game removes it.

4. Accounts and Login

Frog Run does not require an account, registration, or login to play.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Frog Run does not use analytics services, advertising trackers, or other third-party tracking technologies to monitor players.

6. Advertising

Frog Run does not display advertisements and does not use advertising networks or advertising SDKs.

7. Internet and Third-Party Services

Frog Run does not require an online account or a backend service to provide its core gameplay. The Game does not send personal player information to external servers or third parties.

The Game's Settings screen contains links to the developer's Instagram and Facebook pages. If you tap these links, they open in your browser or the respective app, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Frog Run does not share any data with them when you open these links.

8. Device Permissions

Frog Run does not request access to sensitive device features such as the camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location. The Game may use your device's vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off at any time in Settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Frog Run does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the Game does not require personal information to play, players of all ages can enjoy it without submitting any personal data.

10. Data Security

Since Frog Run does not collect personal information, there is no player database maintained by the developer. Your locally saved game progress is protected by your device's own security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
